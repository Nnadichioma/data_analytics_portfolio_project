# Restaurant Insights in Mexico

This project explores **restaurant and consumer data from Mexico** to uncover insights about **customer preferences, restaurant performance, and market gaps**. The dataset includes detailed information about **restaurant cuisine, location, pricing, ratings**, as well as **consumer demographics and preferred cuisines**.  

The goal is to **clean, analyze, and visualize the data** to answer questions about the **highest-rated restaurants, consumer preferences, demand-supply gaps**, and what makes a good restaurant investment.

---

## Dataset

The dataset was obtained from **Kaggle**:  
[Restaurant & Consumer Data (MavenAnalytics)](https://mavenanalytics.io/data-playground/restaurant-ratings)

---

## Tools & Workflow

- **Excel (`Excel/`)** – Data cleaning, handling missing values, creating derived columns (e.g., age brackets, ratings), and pivot table analysis.  
- **SQL (`MexicoRestaurant/`)** – Queries for aggregating, ranking restaurants, and analyzing consumer preferences.  
- **Power BI (`Mexico.Restaurant.Dashboard/`)** – Interactive dashboards showing top restaurants, cuisine ratings, demand-supply gaps, and consumer demographics.  
- **Visuals (`MexicoRestaurantDashboard/`)** – Snapshot image of the dashboard.  

*Workflow: SQL → Excel → Pivot Tables → Power BI → Visuals.*

---

## Key Insights

- **Top-Rated Restaurants:** Highest-rated restaurants include *Emilianos*, *Michiko*, and *Las Mañanitas*.  
- **Cuisine Trends:** Japanese and Coffee Shop cuisines received the **highest ratings**, while Mexican cuisine shows the **largest demand-supply gap**, highlighting market opportunities.  
- **Consumer Demographics:** Majority of customers are **young adults**, **students**, and **unmarried**, with strong preferences for Mexican, Japanese, and Coffee Shop cuisines.  
- **Demand-Supply Gaps:** Mexican cuisine had the largest gap (97 demand vs 28 supply), followed by American and Pizzerias.  
- **Investment Recommendations:** High ratings, premium pricing, franchise potential, and available valet parking are key factors when considering new restaurant investments.
