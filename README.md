# 📊 Data Analytics Portfolio

Welcome to my **Data Analytics Portfolio**, showcasing end-to-end analytics projects from **data cleaning, SQL analysis, Excel workflows, to Power BI dashboards**. Each project demonstrates how I transform raw data into actionable insights.

---

## 🧠 Projects Overview

### 1️⃣ Restaurant Insights in Mexico
**Folder:** `MexicoRestaurant`  
**Objective:** Analyze restaurant performance, customer preferences, and market gaps in Mexico.  
**Tools:** Excel, SQL, Power BI  
**Key Insights:** Japanese and Coffee Shop cuisines had the highest ratings; Mexican cuisine has the largest demand-supply gap; young adults and students dominate the customer base.  
[📂 Explore Project](./MexicoRestaurant)

---

### 2️⃣ Dano Airlines Passenger Satisfaction
**Folder:** `DanoAirlines`  
**Objective:** Identify key factors affecting passenger satisfaction and recommend improvements.  
**Tools:** Excel, SQL, Power BI  
**Key Insights:** On-time flights have highest satisfaction; young adults and students are the main passengers; opportunities to improve check-in, boarding, and in-flight comfort.  
[📂 Explore Project](./DanoAirlines)

---

### 3️⃣ NYC Motor Vehicle Collisions Analysis (Jan–Aug 2020)
**Folder:** `NycCollision`  
**Objective:** Analyze NYC traffic collision data to understand trends, high-risk locations, and causes.  
**Tools:** Excel, SQL, Power BI  
**Key Insights:** Collisions peak in late spring; Belt Parkway is the highest-risk street; driver inattention and unsafe speed are leading factors.  
[📂 Explore Project](./NycCollision)
